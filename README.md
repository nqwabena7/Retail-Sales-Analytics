NovaMart Retail Ltd is a fictional nationwide retail company operating across multiple regions and cities. The company sells products in Electronics, Fashion, Groceries, and Home Appliances.

Management observed fluctuations in sales performance and wanted a deeper understanding of revenue, profitability, regional performance, customer behavior, and salesperson productivity.

As the Data Analyst, my responsibility was to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and develop an interactive dashboard to support executive decision-making.
